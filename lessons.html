Every Lesson

What a Typical Lesson Looks Like

We Start With Comfort & Safety

Before any swimming skill is introduced, we build water comfort and review basic safety rules. For nervous or hesitant children, this phase can take as long as it needs to — there's no rush.

  • Water entry and exit safely
  • Floating on back and front
  • Breath control and blowing bubbles
  • "Stop, don't run" and pool-edge rules

Skills Build Lesson by Lesson

Each session builds on the last. We track your child's progress and adapt each lesson to push them just far enough — challenging without overwhelming.

  • Kicking technique and arm strokes
  • Freestyle, backstroke, and more
  • Treading water and independent swimming
  • Turning, diving, and underwater skills

We Always End on a Win

Every lesson wraps with a positive moment — a skill mastered, a goal reached, or just a fun game in the water. Kids leave excited for next time, not exhausted or discouraged.

  • Recap of what was practiced
  • A quick progress note to share with parents
  • Goals set for the next session
  • Smiles mandatory
Ages & Levels

Who We Teach

Toddlers & Young Children

School-Age Beginners

Ages 6–12. Learning to float, kick, and swim independently for the first time.

Intermediate Swimmers

Teens & Adults
